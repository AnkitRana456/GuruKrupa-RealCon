Where Luxury Meets the Sea

Gurukrupa Realcon's newest project in Bandra Bay, Mumbai, epitomizes luxury and exclusivity with its "One- Of-One" philosophy—offering 3 and 4 bed deck residences in a striking 30-storey, Manhattan-inspired tower designed by European architecture. The development stands out with features like double-heighted lobbies, 10.5 ft. ceilings, large running decks for every home, and curated amenities including a 7,500 sq. ft. rooftop infinity pool, state-of-the-art gym, banquet hall, and 24/7 concierge service. Situated at a coveted address that ensures excellent connectivity via the Coastal Road, Sea Link, and Metro Line-2A, residents enjoy proximity to Mumbai’s best dining, schools, healthcare, and business hubs. The project promises not just refined living but significant investment growth, capitalizing on government-driven infrastructure initiatives and Bandra Bay’s emergence as an elite residential destination.

Prime Bandra Bay Address with Seamless Citywide Connectivity

The project enjoys a prime Bandra Bay, Bandra West location with quick access to Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Coastal Road, Metro connectivity and Western Express Highway, ensuring fast commute across Mumbai. It sits close to Bandstand and Carter Road promenades, top schools, hospitals, dining and BKC, making it a highly convenient and aspirational address for both lifestyle and investment.

An Ensemble of Stunning Residences

The project offers large-format 3 and 4 bed deck residences in a premium high-rise tower, with generous floor-to-ceiling height, spacious running decks, and low-density planning with limited apartments per floor, designed to maximize light, ventilation, privacy, and views for luxury family living in Bandra Bay.
